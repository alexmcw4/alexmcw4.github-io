Alexander Douglas McWeeney

I am a mathematician specializing in differential geometry and geometric analysis. My research focuses on the study of minimal hypersurfaces, the Calabi-Yau conjecture, and related topics in higher-dimensional geometry. I am particularly interested in understanding the geometric and topological properties of minimal surfaces and their applications in mathematical physics.
